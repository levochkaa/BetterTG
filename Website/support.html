Support

BetterTG is an open-source, unofficial Telegram client for iPhone, made by Lev Poznyakov. It is not affiliated with Telegram.

Contact

  • Report a bug or ask a question in GitHub issues.
  • Or write to levochkaaa@icloud.com.
  • Report a security problem privately through GitHub security advisories.

Leave other people's names, messages and phone numbers out of reports and screenshots.

Signing in

BetterTG has no account of its own: sign in with the phone number of your Telegram account. Telegram sends the code to the Telegram app on your other devices, or by SMS. For problems with the account itself, such as a code that doesn't arrive or a ban, contact Telegram support.

Notifications

Notifications arrive through BetterTG's push server, which the Privacy Policy describes. If they don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for BetterTG in iOS Settings and that Notifications via BetterTG Server is on in Settings → Notifications and Sounds. Turning that setting off deletes your device's data from the server; BetterTG then shows notifications only while it is open.

Deleting your account

Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete Account Now deletes your Telegram account with all its messages and contacts. Under If Away For you can also have Telegram delete it after a period without activity. Deleting BetterTG from your iPhone doesn't delete the account.

Ads

BetterTG has no ads of its own. Large public channels show Telegram's sponsored messages, as every Telegram app does.
