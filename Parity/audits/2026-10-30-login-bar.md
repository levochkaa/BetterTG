# Login bar — 2026-10-30

Scope: the login bar from `goal.md`, from `3d309cf0` on `t3/refresh-goal-prompt`.
Implementation is in this audit's commit. Authentication flow and form layout are not rewritten.

## Reference and change

Telegram `telegram-upstream/master`, `fb0e62ba8a147f0821da6aa63c09da7d79a10111`:

- `submodules/AuthorizationUI/Sources/AuthorizationSequenceController.swift`: shared navigation theme.
- `AuthorizationSequencePhoneEntryController.swift` and `AuthorizationSequenceCodeEntryController.swift` in
  that directory: navigation controls and the existing cancel-verification confirmation.
- `submodules/Display/Source/NavigationBar.swift` and
  `submodules/TelegramUI/Components/NavigationBarComponent/Sources/NavigationButtonComponent.swift`:
  glass navigation, 44 pt items and 12 pt text ends.

Before: standalone blue Back and Accounts buttons, reproduced with an isolated instance of the real login view.
After: the existing `TelegramClassicNavigationChrome` supplies the full-width light background, a 44 pt Back
circle and an Accounts text capsule, 16 pt from the sides and with the common 10 pt bottom gap. Accounts remains
a BetterTG action. Both real buttons live inside the glass and retain their existing targets and source guards.
The test-server label is the bar's centred custom title. An empty bar is hidden when there are no controls or label.

`render()` continues to derive visibility and enabled state from `LoginAuthorizationSession`. The same custom
button instances survive updates, so Accounts keeps its action-sheet anchor and repeated auth renders do not
add duplicate visible controls. No TDLib client, account storage or authorization state-machine code changed.

## Open finding and limits

in the full safe area and has no keyboard avoidance or scrolling. Reproduced in the final fixture; fix this next
as a separate form-layout slice, preserving the auth controls and stage ownership.

Real account selection/authentication, server switching, Dynamic Type, RTL, iPad/rotation, iOS 27 and physical
devices were not tested. Other chrome gaps remain: invite-link sheet, link spinner, network usage segmented
control, contact send bar and sticker-settings toolbar.
